Research interests

Software is how we do science. These are the five areas the tools serve; the full picture is on livermetabolism.com.

Digital Twins

Mechanistic, patient-specific models spanning molecule to whole body that mirror an individual's physiology, enabling simulation-based prediction of disease progression and treatment response.

Read more on livermetabolism.com

AI

Machine learning combined with mechanistic modeling to extract patterns from complex biomedical data and support predictive, personalized decision-making in medicine.

Read more on livermetabolism.com

Digital Pathology

Computational and AI-driven analysis of whole-slide histology images to quantify tissue structure, zonation, and disease patterns at scale.

Read more on livermetabolism.com

Pharmacometrics

Physiologically based pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic (PBPK/PD) modeling of drug absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion to support precision dosing.

Read more on livermetabolism.com

Open & FAIR

Open, FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable), and reproducible models, data, and software that the research community can build on.

Read more on livermetabolism.com

PK-DB

Pharmacokinetics database

The first FAIR-compliant open database for pharmacokinetics, integrating clinical and pre-clinical trial data. PK-DB enables reproducible PBPK/PD modeling and individualized simulations and has become a key infrastructure for computational pharmacology research.
